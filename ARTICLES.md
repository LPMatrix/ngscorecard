# Publication Ideas

Editorial ideas for /publications, tracked the way FEATURES.md tracks product
ideas. An entry here is a pitch, not a commitment — see PRINCIPLES.md and the
first published piece for the voice: concrete, sourced, built around one real
pattern found in the data, not a methodology explainer.

## Ideas

- **₦195 billion, or $200 billion?** — a Rivers State flagship project
  circulated in public reporting with its currency wrong by roughly 300x
  (larger than Nigeria's entire federal budget). Short, single-example piece
  about how casually large numbers move through Nigerian infrastructure
  reporting.

- **One feature story, eight flyovers** — the supply-side companion to
  "Which seven-point agenda?": why infrastructure delivery is hard to check
  at all. A single Ogun newspaper feature is the only source for eight named
  projects; one academic PDF is the sole citation for six federal road
  projects spanning two presidencies; a Bayelsa bridge story turns out to be
  bylined by the governor's own press secretary. Not that these are lies —
  most checked out — but that often exactly one document stands between
  "we know this happened" and "we don't," and it's frequently the
  government's own account of itself. Needs another research pass to firm
  up examples.

- **Money in, road out?** — pairs indicator data (public debt, IGR) against
  verified physical delivery in the Projects tab. Needs Projects coverage to
  be deeper and more even across states before it's fair. The hard,
  valuable version of "did the money build anything," and something only
  this dataset is positioned to answer once it's ready.

## Published

- **The bridge everyone commissions** (2026-09-27) — infrastructure credit
  follows the ribbon, not the money: the PH Ring Road (Wike → Fubara), the

  Tinubu in 2026), plus the Wike-Fubara rivalry and Rivers's 2025 state of
  emergency running alongside it the whole time.
  `/publications/the-bridge-everyone-commissions`
