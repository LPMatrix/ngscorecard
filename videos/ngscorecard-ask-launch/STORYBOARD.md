## Video direction

**Ground**: deep forest green (#132a1d) for Frames 1–2 and 7; cream (#fffef9) for Frame 3–4; light gray (#f9f9f9) for Frames 5–6. The first transition from dark to light (Frame 2→3) is the product's visual reveal — it mirrors the emotional shift from pain to relief.

**Type**: Georgia serif throughout. Display weights for hooks and taglines (96px); body weight for eyebrows and UI reconstructions. Cream on green; deep ink on cream. No script, no decorative — editorial authority.

**Rhythm**: Frames 1–4 cut hard and fast (kinetic type drives the beat). Frame 5 is the pivot — a UI reconstruction, paced slower. Frames 6–7 breathe; the hold IS the payoff.

**Motion style**: discrete, intentional, resolved. Each reveal lands and holds. No continuous floating, no ambient drift in hero frames. Stillness after a slam reads as confidence.

**Accent green (#3fbf82)**: used twice — the diagonal stroke in the crest (every frame), and the URL on the end card. Rationed.

**Transitions**: hard cuts within the hook+pain sequence (Frames 1→2); zoom-through at section boundaries (pain→brand, demo→end card); crossfade + blur-crossfade inside the demo sequence.

**Music**: a confident civic minimal underscore — sparse piano, light percussion, deliberate tempo (~90 BPM). Should feel like accountability reporting, not a startup ad. Enters on Frame 1; swells slightly on Frame 6 (the answer reveal); final bar lands on Frame 7.
