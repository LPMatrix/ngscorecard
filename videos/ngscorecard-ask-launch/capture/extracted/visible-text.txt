Subtitle: "Answers come from the Government Service Portal and NHREC, with the sources shown."

Corpus:

Flow:
1. User types a question in the input bar at the bottom
2. User clicks "Ask"
3. Spinner shows "Searching documents..."
4. Answer appears in a white card in the conversation area
5. Sources are shown below the answer: numbered [1] [2], with badge (e.g. "CAC"), document title, and source metadata

Example question: "How do I register a business in Nigeria?"
Example answer: "To register a business in Nigeria, apply to the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC). Start by reserving a business name on the CAC portal, then submit form CAC 2 (for a business name) or CAC 7 (for a company) along with the prescribed filing fee. For a private limited company you also need a Memorandum and Articles of Association. Once approved you receive a Certificate of Incorporation or RC Registration Certificate, usually within 1–5 working days online."
Example sources: [1] CAC — Business Registration Process; [2] CAC — CAC (Company Registration)
