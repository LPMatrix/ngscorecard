## Video Direction

### Pacing & Tone
- Measured, authoritative. Not urgent or promotional in tone.
- Visual language is clean, data-forward, civic in feel.
- Motion is subtle and purposeful, not decorative.

### Color & Typography
- Dark/light contrast (code-editorial preset): dark backgrounds with light text for emphasis.
- Playfair Display for headlines (authority).
- Instrument Sans for body/stats (clarity).

### Music & Sound
- Ambient background music: warm, grounded, not corporate or uplifting.
- Optional subtle SFX: card-open sound on promise cards, filter-click on filter activations.
- Narration: clear, measured, confident.
