## The Pitch

NGScorecard is independent civic infrastructure that makes government accountability data accessible and auditable. For funders, this is foundational work: without transparent, sourced records of what leaders promised versus what actually happened, there's no ground truth to build on.

**What makes it fundable:**
- **Independence** — no government or party money; editorial ratings are the irreplaceable asset
- **Durability** — built on open standards, open data, encourages mirroring; designed to outlast any maintainer
- **Scale** — 5 presidents, 36 states (current + former), 20+ categories per administration
- **Trust** — every rating is dated, sourced, linked to originals; methodology is public and versioned

## Story Arc

**Beat 1: The Problem**
Governments make promises. Citizens need to know if those promises are kept. But there's no single source of truth — no independent, auditable record.

**Beat 2: The Solution**
NGScorecard is that record. For every president and governor in democratic Nigeria, we track: campaign promises, inherited challenges, executive orders, fraud cases, ministerial performance, budgets, and economic outcomes.

**Beat 3: How It Works**
Show the platform: filtered views, the data cards, status ratings (kept · partial · broken · in progress). Show that every entry is sourced and dated. Emphasize the rigor.

**Beat 4: Why It Matters**
This data is used by journalists, researchers, and voters to hold their government accountable. It's durable — open licensed, mirrored, designed to survive. It's the civic infrastructure Nigeria needs.

**Beat 5: Call to Action**
Support independent accountability infrastructure. NGScorecard is built to last.

## Key Visuals

- Landing page hero (introduction)
- A specific administration profile (e.g., Tinubu) showing promises breakdown
- Filtered view (showing status filter in action)
- A promise card expanded (showing source, date, assessment)
- The /guide or /developers page (showing methodology is public)
- A closed loop: promise → outcome → verification

## Audio & Pacing

- Narration: confident, measured tone — this is serious civic work
- Music: ambient, warm but grounded (not corporate, not uplifting; authoritative)
- SFX: subtle interactions (card open, filter activate) to punctuate story beats
- Pacing: Each beat gets ~15 seconds; visuals reveal progressively as narration unfolds

## Success Criteria

A funder watches this and thinks:
- "This is real, independent infrastructure."
- "The data is trustworthy because they show their work."
- "This will outlast any trend; it's built to last."
- "Journalists and voters actually rely on this."

**Not:** "That's a neat web app." (It's infrastructure, not a product.)
