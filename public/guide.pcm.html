1 Wetin dey count as promise wey we fit track

Na di same test we dey use for every president and governor, so di count for one scorecard mean di same thing as di count for anoda one.

Included
Clear promise wey di officeholder or im campaign talk before or as di term dey start — from manifesto, di swearing-in speech, official agenda paper (di Renewed Hope Agenda, di Seven-Point Agenda, and so on), budget speech, or promise wey plenty news carry dat time. E must dey concrete enough make person fit check am: a target, a project, a reform wey get name.
Tracked separately
Wahala wey di administration no cause but wey dem gree publicly say na dem go fix, dey show under Inherited Fixes — we dey rate am for di same four-point scale, but we keep am apart from campaign promise.
Excluded
Big grammar wey no get anything wey person fit measure inside; talk wey person just yarn wey no be part of any real plan; promise wey party people wey no hold di office talk; and things wey dem talk after di time just to describe policy wey don dey ground before.
Selection within a term
We dey follow di promise wey matter pass and wey carry weight, across a fixed set of area — economy, security, governance, infrastructure, power, health, education, and rights. Where di agenda get official pillar, we dey rate against dem pillar instead of picking only di part wey go make dem look fine.

Say promise hard to grade no be reason to comot am — na reason to mark am In progress and continue to check.

2 How we dey rate promise

Every promise wey we dey track — and every entry for Inherited Fixes — dey get one of four rating, based on di evidence wey link to am.

Kept
Di thing wey dem promise really happen, for around di size wey dem promise, and official record or independent news wey gree confam am. Announcement or launch no be "Kept" — na when dem deliver.
Partial
Progress dey wey get proof — money comot, project dey go, dem bring bill, improvement wey person fit measure but wey never complete — wey no reach di size, di reach, or di time wey dem promise.
Broken
Di term, or di promise im own deadline, don pass and nothing serious happen; or dem openly drop di promise; or di result wey we measure move di opposite way.
In progress
Di time wey dem talk never pass and work dey really go on. We dey use am small-small and we dey check am every review cycle — e no be place wey promise go just sidon forever. For raw data dem dey call am "Pending".

When source dem no gree, na di one wey independent pass and wey recent pass we dey follow, and we go write di disagreement for di assessment. If evidence no dey, we no dey take am as failure: if nothing dey wey person fit confam any way and di deadline never pass, di rating go remain In progress; if di deadline don pass and no sign say anything happen, dat one na Broken.

3 Status words for each category

Every oda category get im own status words, because "Kept vs Broken" no fit court case or appointment.

Fraud case

Ongoing
Investigation or prosecution still dey go on.
Dismissed
Dem drop di charge or throw di case comot.
Acquitted
Court find di person say e no guilty.

Every fraud case also carry one separate government response rating — Pursuing, Stalled, Politicised, Abandoned, or No interference — wey dey describe how di government imsef handle di matter, apart from wetin court decide.

Partial

Pending

4 Budget words

Where di figure no dey public, we go leave di space empty instead of guess am — check Source & check.

Total budget
Di full money wey dem approve for di year.
Revenue (projected)
Di money wey di budget assume say go enter, wey dem set as dem pass di budget.
Revenue (actual)
Wetin dem really collect, once dem report am officially.
Deficit
Di gap between wetin dem spend and wetin dem collect, wey borrow dey usually cover.
Debt service
Di part of di budget wey dem dey use service debt wey dem owe before, before dem fund anything else.
Implementation rate
Di share of di budget wey dem approve wey dem really spend or release.
Allocation vs. Released
Per ministry: Allocation na wetin dem budget give am; Released na wetin dem really pay out.

6 Source & check

Every entry — promise, fraud case, budget line, order — get date and link to im original source. We no dey rate anything without reference wey link to am, and di rating na judgment wey person make against dat evidence, no be something wey machine calculate.

Wetin dey count as source
Primary & official records
Government gazette and budget paper, data from di NBS, CBN and DMO, court filing and judgment, official agency report. Na di strongest base for a rating.
Established independent reporting
Naija and international news house wey get editorial standard and public way to correct mistake.
Named expert & civil-society research
Analysis from body like BudgIT or transparency organisation — we dey use am for context and to cross-check di records for top.
Not used on its own
Anonymous social media, one-side press release, and government or opposition claim wey person no fit confam on im own. Government fit talk im own claim and we go quote am, but we go mark am as a claim, no be as confirmation.
About figures

NGScorecard no dey publish guess wey dem dress like fact. Where dem never publish official figure — like state budget line wey never comot — we go leave di space empty instead of put number wey we calculate. Empty mean "dem never publish am", e no mean "zero".

How often we dey check

Every administration carry one reviewed date: di last time we check every entry under am against di evidence wey dey ground now. Administration wey still dey power, we dey check am again on rolling basis — at least two times a year, and after big event like a budget, an election, or a serious court ruling. Administration wey don comot, we go freeze am for im last review unless new evidence show.
